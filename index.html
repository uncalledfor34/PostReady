A fill-in guard shouldn't have to guess.

When someone calls out, the replacement often arrives at a building they've never seen. They don't know which doors lock at night, where the alarm panel is, or who to call for the elevator.

Supervisors can't be at every post for every shift change. Binders get skipped. PostReady puts the site knowledge right at the post, in a short video the guard can watch on their own phone.

How it works

Four steps from site visit to ready guard

01

We film on site

We walk the building with your supervisor and record every part of the post.

02

Narrated, step by step

A clear voiceover explains exactly what the guard does at each spot, on each round.

03

QR code at the post

You get a printable QR code for the desk. No app, no login — the guard scans and watches.

04

Guard confirms

The guard tells their supervisor they watched it. Simple, and nothing extra to manage.

What's in a video

Your post orders, shown instead of written.

Each video is built for one building and one post, so the guard sees the real doors, panels and routes they'll be responsible for.

  • Entrances & access
    Which doors open when, and visitor sign-in.
  • Patrol rounds
    The route, checkpoints and timing.
  • Alarm & fire panels
    Where they are and what to do.
  • Emergency procedures
    Evacuation routes and first steps.
  • Key contacts
    Building manager, engineer, your office.
  • Site-specific rules
    Deliveries, tenants, logs and keys.
