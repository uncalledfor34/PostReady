Harbor Point Tower

100 Demo Plaza · New York, NY
Post: Lobby Command Desk · Overnight 11p–7a

1Badging through the turnstiles

🎥 Turnstile video

Tap the badge flat on the reader in the glass top. Wait for the lights, then walk through to the elevator bank. Only one person per tap.

2Key locations

📷 Main entrance

Main Entrance

Revolving doors and side doors. Check that they're locked after hours, and log it every round.

📷 Lobby desk

Lobby Command Desk

Your post. You'll find the desk phone, badge printer and visitor check-in computer here.

📷 Turnstiles

Turnstiles & Elevator Bank

No one goes past the turnstiles without a badge or a visitor pass.

📷 CCTV station

CCTV Station

Watch the entrance, elevator and dock cameras between rounds.

📷 Fire command panel

Fire Alarm Command Center

It's beside the desk. Do not silence or reset an alarm unless FDNY or the Fire Safety Director tells you to.

📷 Radio charger

Radios & Emergency Plan

Radios charge in the cabinet. The Emergency Action Plan binder and a fire extinguisher are right next to them.

📷 Key lockbox

Key Lockbox

Building keys are kept here. Your supervisor gives you the code in person. Never write it down or share it.

📷 Daily log

Daily Log Book

Write in ink and use 24-hour times. Log when you come on duty, every round, and every vendor.

3Shift duties

11:00pRelieve the evening guard and write "on duty" in the log. Take a radio from the charger and check the keys.
HourlyWalk the lobby, check the entrances and the turnstiles, and write "Lobby safe and secured" in the log.
OvernightWhen a vendor comes (trash pickup, deliveries), log the time they arrive and the time they leave.
Between roundsWatch the CCTV and check in any late visitors.
6:45aPut your radio back on the charger, brief the guard relieving you, and sign off in the log.

4Post rules

  • Every visitor shows photo ID and is checked in on the visitor system before getting a pass.
  • No one goes past the turnstiles without a badge. No exceptions.
  • Keep your radio on you whenever you leave the desk.
  • Write every incident in the log with the time, the names and what happened.
  • Never share codes, passwords or tenant information.

⚠ Emergencies

  • Fire alarm: call 911, notify the Fire Safety Director, meet FDNY at the lobby.
  • Medical: call 911, send someone to meet EMS at the front door.
  • Suspicious person or package: do not touch it, keep people back, call the supervisor.
