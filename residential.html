Every building runs differently.

When a relief doorman covers a shift, they're guessing: where do packages go, who can go up without a call, which hours the freight elevator runs, what to do when the fire alarm sounds.

Residents notice every mistake. PostReady Residential puts your building's procedures right at the desk, in a short video staff can watch on their own phone before they take over.

What we cover

Your building's way of doing things, shown on video.

Each video is built for one building, so staff see the real package room, intercom, freight entrance and alarm panel they'll be working with.

  • Packages & deliveries
    Where they go, what to refuse, perishables.
  • Visitors & contractors
    Who gets announced, check-in and sign-out.
  • Move-ins & freight
    Freight elevator hours, padding, booking rules.
  • Intercom, keys & fobs
    The desk system, cameras and key control.
  • Emergencies
    Fire alarm panel, leaks, after-hours contacts.
  • Concierge services
    Amenity bookings, dry cleaning, car service.

Your residents' privacy stays protected.

Videos cover procedures only — never resident names, unit numbers or personal details. Resident information stays with your building.
